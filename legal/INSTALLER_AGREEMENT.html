B. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

1. What the App processes

Depending on use, RLE Client may process:

  • Instrument connection details (IP/host, nickname) and save-folder paths you choose
  • Sign-in usernames and session tokens, stored securely via the OS credential store where available
  • Report, audit, member/profile, and recipe data retrieved from instruments you authorize
  • Local preferences and device lists in the App user-data directory

2. Purposes

Processing is for connecting and authenticating to Kiosk Devices, downloading/exporting authorized files, remembering your settings, optional scheduled backup to your folders, and securing sessions.

3. Local-first design

By default the App communicates with Kiosk Devices on networks you configure and saves files to folders you choose. It does not create a Raise Lab Equipment cloud account as part of a standard install and does not sell personal information.

4. Sharing

Data is shared with the Kiosk Devices you select and with the OS credential store for token security. Your organization’s network tools may observe traffic according to local policy.

5. Retention and your choices

Local settings remain until you remove them or uninstall. Downloaded files remain in your folders under your control. You may sign out, remove devices (clearing related tokens), delete exports, or uninstall the App.

For license or privacy questions, contact Raise Lab Equipment via the support channels provided with your instrument or commercial agreement. Full text copies are also included with the product under the legal folder (EULA.txt and PRIVACY_POLICY.txt).
